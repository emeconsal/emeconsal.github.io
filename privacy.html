Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026.

openGym backup is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uploads backup archives of the owner's own openGym data to the owner's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

It asks Google only for the drive.file permission. With it, the tool can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

What it collects and shares

Nothing. It collects no personal information, sends no data to anyone other than Google Drive, uses no analytics or tracking, and shares nothing with third parties. The Google sign-in token stays on the owner's own computer.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
